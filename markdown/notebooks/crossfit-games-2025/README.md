# 2025 CrossFit Games

Held August 1–3, 2025 at MVP Arena in Albany, New York — a new city and venue after a single year in Fort Worth. Jayson Hopper won the men's title and Tia-Clair Toomey claimed a record eighth championship; CrossFit Oslo Kriger won the team competition.
