# 2021 CrossFit Games

Held July 27–August 1, 2021 at the Alliant Energy Center in Madison, Wisconsin — the return of a full in-person field after the pandemic-shortened 2020 season. Justin Medeiros won his first men's title and Tia-Clair Toomey her fifth consecutive, tying the then-record for dominance in the women's division.
