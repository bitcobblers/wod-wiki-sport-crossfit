# 2024 CrossFit Games

Held August 9–11, 2024 at Dickies Arena in Fort Worth, Texas — the only Games at that venue after six years in Madison. James Sprague won the men's title and Tia-Clair Toomey returned from her 2023 absence to win a record-extending seventh championship; Raw Iron CrossFit Mayhem Thunder won the team competition. The opening day's swim event was marred by the death of athlete Lazar Đukić, and several athletes withdrew in the days that followed.
