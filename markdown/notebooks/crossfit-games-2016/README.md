# 2016 CrossFit Games

The 10th edition, held July 19–24, 2016 — the first day back at the Ranch in Aromas, California, and the remainder at the StubHub Center in Carson. Mat Fraser won his first individual title, ending the Froning era (Froning had moved to teams), and Katrin Tanja Davidsdottir defended her women's title.
