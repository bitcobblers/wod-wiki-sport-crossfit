# 2022 CrossFit Games

Held August 3–7, 2022 at the Alliant Energy Center in Madison, Wisconsin. Justin Medeiros and Tia-Clair Toomey both repeated their 2021 titles; CrossFit Mayhem Freedom won the team competition.
