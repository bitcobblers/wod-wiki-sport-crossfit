# 2026 CrossFit Games

The 20th edition, held July 22–26, 2026 — a return to California, with the opening day at the original Ranch in Aromas and the remainder of the week at the SAP Center in San Jose. The 20 scored events were the most of any Games. James Sprague defended his men's title, Aimee Cringle won her first women's championship, and CrossFit Whip won the team competition.
