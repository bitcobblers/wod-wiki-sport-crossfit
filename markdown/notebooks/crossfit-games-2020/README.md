# 2020 CrossFit Games

Reformatted for the COVID-19 pandemic into two stages: Stage One was held online in September 2020, and the in-person final stage returned to the CrossFit Ranch in Aromas, California on October 23–25. The events in this notebook are from the in-person final stage. Mat Fraser won his record fifth consecutive title and Tia-Clair Toomey her fourth.

## Events

11 events across 3 competition days. Entries are dated by competition day and sort by event number.
