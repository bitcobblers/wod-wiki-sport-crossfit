# 2018 CrossFit Games

Held August 1–5, 2018 at the Alliant Energy Center in Madison, Wisconsin. Mat Fraser and Tia-Clair Toomey both defended their titles — the second of Fraser's five consecutive championships and Toomey's second of six straight.
